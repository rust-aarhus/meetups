## Making UIs Modular With ddui

by signal_Processor

Monolithic Front-End frameworks are great tools for quickly making applications... until your needs conflict with their architecture. Modularity or composability are typically afterthoughts in the design process, which complicates efforts to modify or reusing existing codebases. What if instead there were crates that could be composed together for creating 2d UI applications?

This talk will explore my attempt to answer this question by creating ddui- a modular set of crates for making applications and audio plugins that work on desktop (both in standalone or child windows) and web. I'll touch on each crate briefly, and explain some of the thoughts and philosophy that went into piecing them together.

I'm a software developer focusing on realtime audio processing, UI, and command line utilities. I've been writing primarily in Rust for about 5 years.
