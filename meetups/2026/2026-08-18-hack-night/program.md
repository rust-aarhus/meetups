# Rust Aarhus Hack Night: Trust but verify the LLM

Date: 2026-08-18

Everyone's talking about AI coding assistants. Fewer are asking hard questions about them.
This Hack Night, we put LLMs to the test - on real Rust code, with real deadlines.
Hear war stories, watch a "live" demo, then grab your laptop and try it yourself.
Bring your skepticism. Bring your subscription if you've got one, we'll pair you up if not.
Where does the machine actually shine - and where does the borrow checker win?

The program for the evening is:

- 18:05 - 18:15: Welcome (10 min)
- 18:15 - 18:40: My LLM journey & demo by Tue Henriksen (25 min)
- 18:40 - 18:50: What is your journey? by YOU (10 min)
- 18:50 - 20:35: Hacking with LLMs
- 20:35 - 20:50: Open discussion (15 min)
- 20:50 - 20:55: Book raffle (5 min)
- 20:55 - 21:00: Closing remarks (5 min)
- 21:00: Let's continue the discussion at a nearby bar
